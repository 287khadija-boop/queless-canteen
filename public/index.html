How Digital Ordering Works

Three simple steps to save your break time and enjoy fresh meals on campus.

1

Browse & Pre-Order

Select your breakfast, lunch, or snacks from the live menu. Choose immediate preparation (ASAP) or schedule a pickup slot.

2

Receive Digital Token

Confirm your order via counter payment or instant online checkout. An atomic, unique token is generated for the kitchen queue.

3

Collect & Enjoy

Track real-time status updates from your phone. Walk up to the pickup counter only when your token is marked "Ready".
